v1.6 fixes weapon-switch loops and blocked tactical routes, gives player and bots shared physical combat rules, and adds actual recoil plus stance/movement accuracy. Rifle damage is 36 body/75 head with a 0.115-second cooldown; sniper is 125/220 with a 1.35-second cooldown. Reloads, ammunition, health, hitboxes, movement limits and physical recoil/spread are shared. Same-step mutual eliminations can draw. Bot controllers still differ from the AI-player controller; equal settings do not guarantee 50% wins.

Hold **Ctrl to crouch**, **Alt to slow walk** for tighter spread. Sprinting/jumping widen it. Recoil affects the shot and camera and recovers over time. AI compensation follows the same bounded turn rate. Shooting still requires sensory contact, aim and readiness. Switching is guarded by commitment and range/ammo need; moving bots can return fire. These are programmed mechanics improvements, separate from learned-policy results.

Exactly **two additional 100-round cycles** trained separate player and bot policies in real Game::step. Both learned, continuing their previous checkpoint. After recoil was added, the same 20 frozen tests were run before training, after 100 and after 200 rounds: **5 wins, 14 losses, 1 mutual elimination, 0 timeouts — 25% each time**. Mean bot damage stayed 48. **No measured win-rate or damage improvement from the extra training.** A replay from the original checkpoints after final parity fixes produced identical raw results/models. These are repeated progress tests against a frozen original AI-player policy, not a human benchmark or a fresh blind holdout.

The default is the final policy labelled **AFTER 200 / RECOIL**. **F5** switches to the exact starting **BEFORE 200 / RECOIL** policy and restarts the round. The policies differ but scored identically. See the [full report, earlier separately labelled phases, raw outcomes and saved models](https://github.com/tffere/tffere-strike/blob/main/training/runs/2026-11-08-v1.6/REPORT.md). The pre-recoil 45%→55% result is a separate experiment and cannot be compared directly to these new rules/seeds.
